## 1. Academic Career

### Typical roadmap

- ✅ Maintain a high CGPA (already done)
    
- Publish **1–3 research papers** (strongly recommended)
    
- Complete an M.Sc. (Bangladesh or abroad)
    
- Gain teaching experience (Teaching Assistant experience helps)
    
- Take IELTS/TOEFL if planning to study abroad
    
- Apply for a fully funded PhD
    
- Become Assistant Professor → Associate Professor → Professor
    

### Mandatory

- Master's degree
    
- Research ability
    
- Publications (especially for competitive universities)
    
- Good communication and teaching skills
    

### Good to do

- Research internships
    
- Conference presentations
    
- Networking with professors
    
- Research grants
    
- GitHub portfolio (important in AI/CS)
    

**Pros**

- Intellectual freedom
    
- Research opportunities
    
- International collaborations
    
- Good work-life balance in many universities
    

**Cons**

- Slow salary growth initially
    
- Competitive PhD admissions
    
- Publish-or-perish pressure
